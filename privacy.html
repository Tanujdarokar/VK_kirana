Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026. This policy describes the current VKCommerce demo website, not a production retail service.

Demo and data storage

VKCommerce is a static demonstration. Profile details, cart contents, orders, wishlist entries, and store-management examples are stored in this browser's local storage. This demo does not provide server-backed accounts, secure authentication, or a production checkout. Do not enter passwords, payment details, or sensitive personal information.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The site records your optional-cookie choice in local storage so the preference banner can remember it. No analytics or advertising tracker is currently configured. Necessary browser storage is used for the demo features regardless of the optional-cookie choice.

Third-party resources

Product photos, fonts, and icons may load from external providers such as Unsplash, Google Fonts, and the Lucide CDN. When your browser requests those resources, those providers may receive technical request information such as your IP address. Their own privacy terms apply to those requests.

Managing and deleting local data

Clear this site's browser storage/site data to remove locally saved demo profiles, shopping data, and cookie preferences. Clearing storage may reset the demo to its sample state.

Production use

Before this project is used as a real store, its operator must replace this demo notice with a legally reviewed policy that describes the actual data controller, purposes, retention, processors, user rights, and contact details.
